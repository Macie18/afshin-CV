中学部秋季学院运动会

SPORTS DAY 2025

#


SPORTS 


秋阳为幕，绿茵为席。在全体师生的热切期盼与积极参与中，无锡协和中学部2025年秋季学院运动会于11月5日圆满落下帷幕。这是一场力与美的盛宴，更是一次心与心的交融。现在，就让我们一起回顾那些不容错过的精彩瞬间！

Beneath the autumn sun and upon the green fields, the Wuxi United International School 2025 Autumn House Sports Day successfully concluded on November 5th, amidst the eager anticipation and active participation of all our students and staff. It was a spectacular showcase of strength and beauty, and a genuine connection of hearts and minds. Let's take a moment to relive the unforgettable highlights!


SPORTS 






朝气蓬勃 精彩开幕


运动会开始前，锡山区欧美同学会（WRSA）向学校进行了体育器材捐赠。这份浓浓的情谊与切实的支持，为孩子们的体育梦想注入了新的动力。

Prior to the commencement of the Sports Day, the Western Returned Scholars Association (WRSA) of Xishan District made a donation of sports equipment to the school. This profound kindness and tangible support have injected new energy into the children's athletic dreams.

随后，运动会在激昂的旋律中拉开帷幕。在啦啦队的带领下，各班级队伍身着色彩鲜艳的学院T恤，迈着整齐有力的步伐，意气风发地步入赛场。他们口号响亮，眼神坚定，充分展现了协和学子蓬勃向上的精神风貌。

 The sports day then officially commenced with the playing of spirited melodies. Teams from each class, dressed in their brightly colored house T-shirts, entered the venue with confident, synchronized steps. Their loud chants and determined eyes fully showcased the vibrant and upward spirit of WUIS students.

开幕式上，联合校长孟晓东先生发表了热情洋溢的致辞。他首先向锡山区欧美同学会（WRSA）向学校捐赠体育器材的善举表达诚挚谢意。孟校长在致辞中强调，体育运动是学校教育不可或缺的重要组成部分，既能强健体魄，更能培养学生坚持不懈的意志品质和团队协作精神。他鼓励同学们在赛场上尽情挥洒汗水、展现自我，既要勇于拼搏、争取佳绩，更要享受运动带来的快乐与成长。

At the opening ceremony, Mr. Meng Xiaodong, Co-Principal of the school, delivered an enthusiastic address. He began by expressing sincere gratitude to WRSA for their benevolent donation of sports equipment to the school. In his speech, Mr. Meng emphasized that sports are an indispensable part of school education, not only strengthening physical health but also fostering perseverance and team spirit among students. He encouraged all students to fully exert themselves on the field, showcase their abilities, and strive for excellence while embracing the joy and personal growth that sports bring.




热血拼搏 闪耀风采


赛场上，运动健儿们无疑是绝对的主角。

The student athletes were undoubtedly the stars of the event.

团队协作的智慧：拔河比赛的齐心协力、飞盘的默契传递、篮球的并肩同行，无不诠释着“团结就是力量”的真谛。

The Wisdom of Teamwork: The unified effort in the Tug-of-War, the seamless coordination in the Frisbee, and the side-by-side cooperation in the Basketball all perfectly illustrated the true meaning of "Unity is Strength."

个人力量的绽放：田径赛道上如风的身影，乒乓球、羽毛球划出的优美弧线，都是力与美最完美的结合。

The Bloom of Individual Talent: The swift figures on the track and the perfect arcs of the ping-pong and badminton showcased the ultimate blend of power and grace.

趣味项目的欢乐：龙舟竞速的呐喊与各种游戏的欢声笑语，让整个赛场变成了欢乐的海洋。

The Joy of Fun Events: The enthusiastic shouts during the Dragon Boat Race and the laughter during the games turned the entire venue into a sea of joy.



每一个身影都是拼搏的注脚，每一声加油都是温暖的鼓励。同学们不仅赛出了水平，更赛出了风格，赢得了荣誉，也收获了友谊。

Every athlete was a testament to determination, and every cheer was a gesture of warm support. Our students not only competed with skill but also with great sportsmanship, earning honors and forging stronger friendships along the way.


共享胜利 高光时刻


经过一天的激烈角逐，各学院的积分榜最终尘埃落定。我们向最终获得最高分、赢得特别奖励的绿色学院表示最热烈的祝贺！掌声同样送给每一位参与者和幕后付出的老师与志愿者，正是所有人的共同努力，才铸就了这场精彩、安全、有序的体育盛会。

After a day of intense competition, the final House points standings were settled. We extend our warmest congratulations to the Green House for ultimately achieving the highest score and winning the special award! Our applause also goes to every participant, and to the teachers and volunteers working behind the scenes – it was your collective effort that made this sports event so spectacular, safe, and well-organized.

汗水辉映青春，拼搏铸就梦想。无锡协和中学部秋季运动会虽已结束，但体育精神永不落幕。让我们将赛场上展现出的拼搏、协作与乐观，延续到今后的学习与生活中，继续携手，共创更多精彩！

Sweat reflects youth; perseverance builds dreams. Although the WUIS Secondary Section Autumn Sports Day has concluded, the spirit of sportsmanship lives on. Let us carry the determination, cooperation, and optimism demonstrated on the field into our future studies and daily lives. Let's continue to work hand in hand to create more wonderful moments together!

期待明年秋日，我们再相约！

We look forward to seeing you all again next autumn!
